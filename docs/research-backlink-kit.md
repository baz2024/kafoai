# Research summary backlink kit

Each paper has a summary page on kafoai.com. Post the short blurb below on the listed profiles and link to the summary page. Most of these links are `nofollow`, but they still help discovery and credibility, and a few (your own ORCID, university and GitHub pages) are strong signals.

Only link to what is public. The first paper is under review, so share the summary page, never the manuscript.

## Paper 1: Agentic, Context-Aware Risk Intelligence in the Internet of Value

Page: https://kafoai.com/research/agentic-risk-intelligence-internet-of-value

> Risk across blockchains comes from a combination of route, sentiment, liquidity and policy, not any single chain. We propose a five-engine architecture (prediction, decentralised verification, sentiment fusion, a rule-bound agent, and a scenario engine) and test it in a 27-hour liquidity stress-response experiment and a 168-hour calibration study. Summary: https://kafoai.com/research/agentic-risk-intelligence-internet-of-value

## Paper 2: Cost-Aware, Self-Adaptive Agentic AI Architecture (preprint)

Page: https://kafoai.com/research/cost-aware-self-adaptive-agentic-ai-architecture
Full PDF: https://papers.omnirisk.io/media/papers/2026/aisoftsys-agentic-risk/manuscript.pdf

> How do you build one AI agent that stays cheap to run and calibrated for months under adversarial inputs? ## Where to post (best first)

1. **ORCID** works list: add both papers with the kafoai.com URL.
2. **TU Dublin staff or research profile**: link both summary pages.
3. **Google Scholar profile**: the pages already carry `citation_*` tags. Scholar mainly indexes PDFs, so paper 2 is eligible now and paper 1 after publication.
4. **arXiv**: if you submit the preprint, put the kafoai.com summary URL in the comments field.
5. **ResearchGate / Academia.edu / Semantic Scholar**: add the summary and link.
6. **LinkedIn and X**: one post per paper using the blurb above.
7. **Paragraph / Medium**: expand the blurb into a short post linking to the summary page.

## Rules

- Do not post summaries of the two papers marked DECOMMISSIONED, the tokenization survey, or the draft summary and position papers until their status changes.
- Update the `paperUrl` in `content/research/<slug>.md` when a paper is published, and add the DOI.
- No paid links, link exchanges or comment spam. They risk a search penalty and don't help.
