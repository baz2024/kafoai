### 3. Kafo AI product ecosystem

Build dedicated product pages for the following six initiatives.

**OmniRisk — AI Risk Intelligence**

Multi-chain digital asset risk intelligence, on-chain analytics, market risk assessment, explainable scoring, predictive analytics and agentic investigations, subject to repository verification.

Do not claim traditional bank credit scoring is operational unless demonstrated.

**AI Wealth — AI Personal Finance Manager**

Personal finance management focused on expense tracking, financial visibility, budgets, savings goals, holdings, intelligent insights and financial planning.

Present it primarily as a personal finance manager, not merely an investment advisory platform.

**RayaChain — Blockchain Credit Intelligence**

IoV-based credit scoring on blockchain, verifiable credit intelligence and decentralised financial trust.

Verify what IoV means in the actual RayaChain project, the architecture, supported chains, current implementation and available demonstrations before publishing detailed claims.

**Circul — Digital Savings Circles**

Arabic-first UAE digital Jam'iya (جمعية), allowing members to organise savings circles, coordinate contributions and manage payout rotations.

The existing sandbox uses simulated banking and payment flows. Clearly separate these from proposed future live integrations.

**Islamic Crypto Lending — Islamic Digital Finance**

Research into Sharia-compliant digital asset financing, blockchain-based financing models and responsible financial innovation.

Do not claim operational lending, approved Islamic financial products or Sharia certification.

**QardX Instant — Embedded Financial Technology**

Financial technology infrastructure and application development, subject to repository verification.

Determine whether this should be a full public product, an R&D initiative, or supporting technology within the Kafo AI ecosystem. Document the recommendation.
